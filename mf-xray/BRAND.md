# MF X-ray — Brand sheet

**Promise:** see through your mutual funds. Beginner-friendly, privacy-first, educational (never advice).
**Tone:** a knowledgeable friend, not a broker. Plain words, no jargon without a tooltip, calm about money.

## Logo
`src/Logo.tsx` (`LogoMark`, `Wordmark`). An indigo rounded square holding an **X** made of one white and one teal stroke (two funds crossing = overlap), with a thin saffron **scan line** (the "x-ray"). The scan line animates in the video. Wordmark: "MF" in white/ink + "X-ray" in teal, Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |

| Ink | `#0E1A3B` | Text, dark scenes, trust |

## Typography
- **Headlines / numbers:** Plus Jakarta Sans 500/700/800 (tabular numerals for money).
- **Body / UI:** Inter 400/500/600.
- Both SIL OFL 1.1, self-hosted in `public/fonts/`.

## Motion language
Springy word-by-word kinetic headlines, numbers that count up, charts that draw left to right, one teal scan-line sweep as the recurring "x-ray" motif, soft fades between scenes.

## Voice
Warm, upbeat Gemini voice "Kore" (see `script.md`). Captions are burned in for sound-off viewing.

## Name suggestion
"MF X-ray" is clear and memorable but "MF" is jargon for beginners and X-ray is also an X (Twitter) pun risk. A friendlier alternative: **"FundLens"** (or "Paisa X-ray"): no abbreviation, still the see-through idea, easy to say in Hindi/English. The video uses **MF X-ray** as requested. Verify trademark/domain availability before adopting.

## Legal/tone rules
Always show "Educational, not investment advice." All figures in the video are fictional sample data (fake persona "Aarav", fake fund names).
